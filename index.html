The brief

Vasool helps people actually use the numberless benefits inside the memberships they already pay for, by showing the right perk at the moment of purchase and turning value recovery into a game.

02 · Problem Definition

People pay for perks, then forget they have them

Problem statement

Urban Indian professionals pay for multiple memberships but use only a fraction of the benefits, because perks are scattered across apps, cards and fine print, and nobody reminds them at the moment a perk could be used.

How might webring the right perk to people at the moment they can use it, and make using it feel rewarding?

09 · High-fidelity screens Delivery

Six screens, one loop, and the decisions behind them

Nudge at checkout

The perk shows up while the cart is open, the only moment it can still be used.

One best perk, not a list

The app picks the single best match and states its ₹ value. No comparing.

"Not now" is easy

Saying no is one tap. Rare, skippable nudges keep trust (assumption 4).

Ask instead of tracking

No bank access, so the user confirms. The saving is editable, so it stays honest.

Motion only for rewards

The app is calm everywhere else. Confetti and counters appear only when value is gained.
